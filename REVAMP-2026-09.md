# Siberforge revamp -- September 2026

Scope agreed with Pavel: full URL migration, new content modules (State of the Cycle,
Policy & Rates, Global/FX/Commodities, Liquidity & Fiscal, This Week), significance-gated
sector tilts, editorial landing page, deletion of obsolete files.

## What was wrong (audit of 90 live pages, 2026-09-26)

1. FRED blocked Vercel's egress IPs (403 on every /api/fred call): most Macro and
   Regional pages were blank. The FRED key was published in supply/data/manifest.json.
2. Structure: six nav sections did not match the URL tree (14 pages under another
   section's path); "Markets" was 45 company pages and 3 market pages; single-name pages
   were 4,500px wide; 30 pages had no <main>, so injected blocks landed after the footer.
3. Analytics: "NY Fed recession probability" was a different model; ICE HY/IG OAS on
   FRED covers only 3 years yet was called a 30-year percentile; hand-picked linear score
   anchors (with a disclosure table that disagreed with the code); regime/return
   look-ahead; COVID-contaminated mean/sd z-scores; conviction measured from the origin;
   sector tilt ranked on raw returns, contradicted its own text and was not significant;
   recession count triple-counted labor; ~15 wrong tooltip facts.
4. Gaps: no summary page, no Fed/rates view, no global/FX/commodities, no liquidity or
   fiscal view, no weekly digest, no breadth/vol.

## What changed

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| Data | `data/fred/` snapshot of ~470 series + release calendar; `lib/fred-snapshot.js` fetch shim on every page; proxy: UA, 4s timeout, 40-id cap, stop-after-403, short cache on partial results; key redaction in pipelines; bad IDs fixed (USCONS, DFII5, FHFA metro-division codes, POPTHM, 4 states without CONS series) |
| IA | Pages moved out of `/core/` into `/macro /markets /regional /supply /research /tools`; 301 redirects for every old path; company picker instead of a 38-link row; runtime `<main>` wrapper |
| Analytics | Percentile/point-in-time composites (one implementation); term-spread probit; Baa-10Y for long credit history; robust regime z + one-month lag; boundary-distance conviction; NW t-stat gated tilts; 4-channel recession count with live calibration table; backtest: lag, T-bill Sharpe, equal-weight benchmark, bootstrap CI verdict |
| New pages | `/macro/` State of the Cycle, `/macro/this-week/`, `/macro/policy/`, `/macro/liquidity/`, `/markets/global/`; Markets overview gets sector scoreboard, breadth and VIX instead of a clickbait news feed |
| Content | Tooltip catalog corrected and re-dated 2026-Q3; page claims fixed; research notes dated with 60-day stale flag; AI screen/Top-5 labelled as April 2026 archives |
| Ops | `snapshot-fred.yml`; all data workflows serialized (`data-commits`) with rebase before push; Node 22; `npm ci` only; CI covers new folders, mojibake check, data-integrity tests |
| Landing | Says what the site is; live verdict and dials in the first screen; editorial (red) skin on the landing only |
