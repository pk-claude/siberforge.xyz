How this site works

Data

  • About 470 FRED series are snapshotted twice each weekday into static files, so pages load fast and keep working when an upstream API is down.
  • Market prices are daily closes; company fundamentals come from SEC EDGAR filings.

Method

  • Composite scores rank each input against its own last 20 years and use only data that had been published at the time.
  • Signals carry their hit rates and false alarms; sector tilts need statistical significance before they are called.
