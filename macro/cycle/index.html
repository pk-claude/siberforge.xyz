How close is the cycle to a turn?

Reading the data…

The regime page says which growth and inflation quadrant the economy is in. This page asks how close it is to a turn. The score combines five signals (curve-based recession odds, the Sahm rule, a corporate credit spread, financial conditions and jobless claims); the four sections below show the detail behind them.

What the score is made of

Which inputs push the score up, which hold it down, and how it moved over three months. A description of the data, not a forecast or a recommendation.

1. Recession signals: one leading, one coincident

Twelve-month-ahead recession odds from a term-spread probit (the NY Fed's yield-curve model, recomputed here from the 10Y–3M spread), with the Chauvet-Piger coincident probability for context. The Sahm Rule triggers once the 3-month average jobless rate rises 0.5pp above its low of the prior 12 months. Both have a recent false alarm: the curve model read above 50% through 2023 and Sahm triggered in July 2024, and no recession followed.

2. Yield curve — 10Y–3M and 10Y–2Y

The 10Y–3M spread is the NY Fed’s preferred recession model input; 10Y–2Y is the financial-press favorite. Every US recession since 1968 was preceded by a 10Y–3M inversion, with lags of roughly 6 to 18 months; the 2022–25 inversion was not followed by one. Recessions have often begun after the curve had already re-steepened.

3. Financial conditions — NFCI + Adjusted NFCI

Chicago Fed composites of ~105 risk, credit, and leverage measures. NFCI is the raw reading (z-scored by construction); ANFCI strips out the part explained by current macro. A gap between the two is worth noting: when ANFCI is above NFCI, conditions are tighter than the state of the economy alone would explain.

4. Credit spreads — HY and IG OAS

A market-priced gauge of default risk. FRED now carries only about three years of the ICE OAS series, so the chart adds the Moody's Baa-10Y spread for long history. HY bands used here: below 300bp tight, 300–500bp normal, 500–800bp elevated, above 800bp stress. When HY widens while IG stays calm, stress is concentrated in weaker borrowers; when both widen together it is broad.

Methodology & sources

Score. Each input is ranked against its own last 20 years, with the direction set so that 100 means the most risk; the score is their weighted average. The inputs, weights and transforms are listed under “How this score is built” next to the score.

Data. Data: FRED (USREC, T10Y3M, SAHMREALTIME, RECPROUSM156N, NFCI, ANFCI, BAA10Y, ICE BofA OAS). Pink bands on time-series = NBER recessions. Read from a static snapshot refreshed twice each weekday.

Limits. History uses today’s revised figures. BLS published no October 2025 CPI or household-survey data; that month is interpolated so year-over-year rates span 12 months. Thresholds quoted in the tiles are reference points from history, not rules. More on sources and method.
