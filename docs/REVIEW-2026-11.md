# Macro section review — November 2026

Date: 2026-11-15. Scope: the 13 pages under `/macro/` plus the shared pieces they depend on
(data shim, composite scores, nav, footer, landing claims). Method: every page read in a
browser on the live site, three independent read-only audits (analytics core, thematic pages,
copy and facts), and node scripts against the FRED snapshot to reproduce numbers. Work is on
branch `macro-review-2026-11`, not committed and not deployed.
